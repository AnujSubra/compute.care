Panayapatti · Established 2025

Where our first chapter began.

SRM Arunachalam Higher Secondary School is home to our first computer lab, inaugurated on October 3, 2025. Students build familiarity through regular lessons, guided practice, and shared projects. A student recognition ceremony on December 24, 2025 celebrated their progress.

Kuruvikondanpatti · Established 2026

A new classroom. More possibilities.

Our second government-aided school program extends TCI’s daily curriculum to Kuruvikondanpatti. The new computer lab, installed in 2026, brings together equipment, local teaching, and the same hands-on approach used at Panayapatti.
