Who we are

Talent is everywhere.
Let’s open the doors.

Where a student grows up shouldn’t determine what they can create. Founded by Anish and Anuj Subramanian, The Compute Initiative brings computer science education to under-resourced, government-aided schools in rural Tamil Nadu.

We work with local teachers, schools, and volunteers to make learning part of everyday school life—from setting up computers and internet access to supporting daily lessons for grades 6–9.

What we do

Access is the beginning.
Confidence is what comes next.

01 / EQUIP

A place to begin

We help schools set up computers, software, and internet access so students can learn by doing.

02 / TEACH

Guidance, every day

Dedicated local teachers guide practice, answer questions, and help students build skills at their own pace.

03 / CREATE

Skills to build on

Students progress from files and email to presentations, practical projects, and programming enrichment.
