Our curriculum

Small steps.
Real skills.

A practical introduction to the digital world, designed for students to learn together and build confidence through doing.

The learning journey

Learn it.
Try it.
Make it yours.

Seven foundational units lead into a review and practical project in week eight. Teachers can adjust the pace so every student gets time to practise.

Teacher-led demonstrations
Paired practice
Hands-on projects
01WEEK 1Introduction to computers+

A confident first click.

Understand hardware and software, turn a computer on and off safely, and practise with the mouse and keyboard.

Try it in class
Take turns with a partner: one student uses the mouse and keyboard, then switch.

02WEEK 2Operating system basics+

Find your way around.

Navigate the desktop, open and close programs, and create, save, and organise files and folders.

Try it in class
Create a folder and save your work where you can find it again.

03WEEK 3Internet basics+

Explore with care.

Open a browser, search for information, and discuss safe browsing and online privacy.

Try it in class
Search for information together and talk about staying safe online.

04WEEK 4Email basics+

Make a connection.

Follow a teacher-led account demonstration and practise sending and receiving email with simple attachments.

Try it in class
Take turns composing a message and adding an attachment.

05WEEK 5Word processing+

Put ideas on a page.

Create and edit a letter or essay, format text with bold, italics, and lists, and save the document.

Try it in class
Write a short letter with a partner and format it so it is easy to read.

06WEEK 6Spreadsheet basics+

Make sense of numbers.

Enter data into a spreadsheet and explore simple calculations, including sum and average.

Try it in class
Enter data in a prepared sheet, then work through a calculation together.

07WEEK 7Presentation basics+

Share what you know.

Create slides, add text and images, and practise presenting to the class.

Try it in class
Build a shared slide deck and give a short presentation.

08WEEK 8Review & practical project+

Bring it all together.

Review the skills learned and complete a mini-project combining a document, spreadsheet, and presentation.

Try it in class
Work in pairs, share the finished project, and receive feedback on your next steps.
