Events & milestones

The moments that
move us forward.

From our first lab to a second school, each step grows from the work of students, teachers, and a wider community.

2026

A second school joins the journey

The computer lab at Kuruvikondanpatti extends our daily computer science program to a second community, with Saranya joining as a local instructor.

2026

300 books. More ways to learn.

TCI distributed 300 books to students at partner schools, supporting reading and learning beyond the computer lab.

2026

Part of the Code Club community

TCI is officially registered as a Code Club, part of the Raspberry Pi Foundation’s global network. Anish and Anuj are recognised as Code Club leaders and mentors.

December 24, 2025

Celebrating student progress

Anish and Anuj joined the SRM Arunachalam staff for a Student Recognition Ceremony in Panayapatti, celebrating students’ progress in computer science since the lab opened.

October 3, 2025

Our first computer lab opens

The Panayapatti inauguration brought together students, educators, community leaders, and S. Ragupathi, who officiated the ceremony. It marked the start of a continuing commitment to computer science learning.
