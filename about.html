Our story

Built by students.
Carried by a community.

Founded by Anish and Anuj Subramanian, The Compute Initiative is a nonprofit bringing computer science education to under-resourced government-aided schools in rural Tamil Nadu.

We bring together fundraising, grassroots volunteer recruiting, hardware and internet installation, school logistics, and teacher support. Local educators turn those resources into daily learning for students in grades 6–9.

Our work began in Panayapatti and has grown to a second school in Kuruvikondanpatti. The goal stays the same: give students the tools, guidance, and time to discover what they can do.
